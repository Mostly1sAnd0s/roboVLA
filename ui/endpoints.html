A node that is busy is not a closed door: it is a queue with a wait. Pick the queue you want to join, then start the run on that endpoint. Nodes you may not pick say why.

How the queue decides

  • Pinned jobs (endpoint named at submit) wait on that machine only.
  • Auto jobs first-fit across every enabled machine that runs the same model family — they are nobody's queue until a slot frees.
  • No preemption: a run-now jump goes to the front of the line after the current job finishes (§3).
  • A train slot and a serving (infer) slot are different pools, so a robot driving on a slot does not block your training run (§3).
